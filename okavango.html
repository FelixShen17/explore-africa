Okavango Delta
奥卡万戈三角洲

非洲最后的原始天堂，水道、湿地与野生动物交织而成的生命奇迹。
A pristine inland delta where water, wildlife and wilderness coexist in harmony.

Experiences · 精选体验

湿地奇观 · Wetland Wonderland

季节性洪水塑造出世界最大的内陆三角洲，水草、泻湖与岛屿不断变化，孕育极其丰富的生态系统。

Seasonal floods shape the world’s largest inland delta, creating ever-changing wetlands rich in biodiversity.

野生动物 · Wildlife Safari

大象、狮子、水牛、河马在这里自由迁徙，是非洲最纯粹的野生动物观赏地之一。

Home to elephants, lions, buffalo and hippos, offering some of Africa’s most authentic wildlife encounters.

独木舟 · Mokoro Canoe

乘坐传统独木舟悄然穿行于水道之间，贴近水面，聆听自然最真实的声音。

Glide silently through narrow channels on a traditional mokoro canoe, immersed in nature.

空中视角 · Aerial View

从空中俯瞰三角洲，水与陆地交织成抽象画卷，是理解奥卡万戈规模与美感的最佳方式。

View the delta from above to fully appreciate its scale and natural artistry.

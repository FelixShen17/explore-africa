Zambezi River Cruise
赞比西河游船

A timeless journey along one of Africa’s greatest rivers.
沿着非洲最伟大的河流之一，开启一段悠然与野性并存的旅程。

Experience · 体验亮点

Sunset Cruise · 日落游船

As the sun sets over the Zambezi, golden reflections dance across the river, creating one of Africa’s most romantic scenes.
当夕阳缓缓沉入河面，金色的光影在赞比西河上流转，构成非洲最迷人的日落画面之一。

Wildlife Encounters · 野生动物邂逅

Hippos, crocodiles, elephants and birds are frequently seen along the riverbanks during the cruise.
河岸两侧常可见河马、鳄鱼、大象与各类鸟类，近距离感受原始非洲生态。

Luxury on Water · 水上轻奢

Comfortable seating, drinks, and attentive service make the cruise both relaxing and refined.
舒适的座椅、精致的饮品与贴心服务，让旅程在野性中不失优雅。

Peaceful Rhythm · 河流的节奏

The gentle flow of the Zambezi offers a peaceful contrast to the thunder of Victoria Falls upstream.
与上游雷鸣般的瀑布不同，赞比西河以宁静的节奏抚慰人心。

Kilimanjaro · 乞力马扎罗

从雨林到雪峰

从热带雨林的葱郁，到高山荒原的辽阔， 再到赤道雪峰的震撼， 乞力马扎罗的每一步， 都是与自然的极致对话。 这不仅是一次登山， 更是一场跨越四季的生命旅程。

From lush tropical rainforests to vast alpine deserts, and finally to the breathtaking snow-capped summit on the equator, every step on Mount Kilimanjaro is an intimate dialogue with nature. This is not merely a climb, but a life journey across four seasons.
